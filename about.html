our story

Why we built OttLoop.

Most "free tool" sites online aren't really free — they bury the actual tool behind ads, countdown timers, or an account wall three clicks in. We got tired of that loop, so we built our own: one place, no detours, the tool does the thing you came for.

OttLoop is being built one category at a time — Image, PDF, Text, Conversion, SEO, Developer, CSS, Calculator, Date & Time, Social Media, YouTube, E-commerce, Security and Education — with the goal of covering the everyday utilities people reach for most, without the noise around them.

Where a tool can run entirely in your browser, it does. That means your files aren't uploaded to a server just to get compressed or converted — they stay on your device, and the tool still gets the job done.

What guides the build

  • Every tool stays free — no premium tier hiding the real feature.
  • No account required to use a single tool on the site.
  • Client-side first — your files don't leave your device unless they have to.
  • New tools ship based on what people actually ask for.
