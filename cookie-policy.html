Cookie Policy

This policy explains what cookies and browser storage OttLoop Tools uses, why we use them, and how you can control or disable them.

01

Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how OttLoop Tools ("OttLoop," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies on tools.ottloop.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers how we handle personal information more broadly.

OttLoop is built to run with as little tracking as possible. We use cookies and storage only where they make the site or a tool actually work better for you.

02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember information about your visit, like your preferences or login state. We also use related browser storage — localStorage and sessionStorage — which work similarly but are typically used to store larger or longer-lived tool settings directly in your browser rather than sending them back to a server.

03

Cookies We Use

We keep our cookie footprint intentionally small. Here's what's set when you use tools.ottloop.com:

CookieTypePurposeDuration
Session stateEssentialKeeps forms and in-progress tool inputs intact as you navigate a single visit.Ends when you close your browser
Preference cookieFunctionalRemembers settings inside individual tools, like your last-used units or output format.Up to 12 months
Security cookieEssentialHelps protect forms against spam and abuse.Short-lived (session)

We do not set advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to build cross-site tracking profiles.

04

Storage Inside Individual Tools

Many OttLoop tools run entirely in your browser and use localStorage to hold your work-in-progress — for example, remembering the last image you cropped or the text you were formatting, so you don't lose it on an accidental refresh. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for tools.ottloop.com, or by using your browser's private/incognito mode.

05

Third-Party Cookies

A small number of third-party services we rely on may set their own cookies or make requests that involve limited technical data:

  • Google Fonts — loads our typefaces; Google may log basic request data (like IP address) as part of serving the font files.
  • Google Apps Script — used only when you submit the newsletter or contact form, to route your submission to us. It does not set tracking cookies.

We do not embed third-party advertising networks, analytics pixels, or social media widgets that set tracking cookies.

06

Managing Cookies

You're always in control of cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:

  • View which cookies are currently stored and delete them individually.
  • Block all cookies from being set, or block third-party cookies specifically.
  • Clear cookies automatically every time you close the browser.

Here's roughly what that looks like for OttLoop's essential and functional cookies — these are illustrative, since actual control lives in your browser's settings, not on this page:

Essential cookies

Required for the site and forms to function. Cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

ALWAYS ON

Functional / preference cookies

Remember tool settings between visits. Safe to disable — tools will just reset to defaults each time.

OPTIONAL

Disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the site — like the contact form — from working correctly.

07

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there isn't a common industry standard for how sites should respond to it, OttLoop does not currently change its behavior based on this signal. Given our minimal cookie use to begin with, this has limited practical effect on your privacy while using the site.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the site evolves and new tools are added. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically.
